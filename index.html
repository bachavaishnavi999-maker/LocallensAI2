🤝 The community layer

This is the feature that turns LocalLens from another map/search interface into a local discovery platform.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Resident tips

Locals can recommend a tiny café, a neighborhood walk, a craft studio, or a seasonal experience.

🛡️ Trust & freshness

Every tip can show contributor type, last-updated date, community reports, and moderator verification.

💬 Local context

Instead of only a pin, visitors see what to try, when to go, what to respect, and what makes it special.

🌱 Local economy

Discovery can give smaller local businesses and community-run experiences visibility.
